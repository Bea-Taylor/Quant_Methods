Practical - logistic and Poisson regression on the schools data

Author

Adam Dennett

Published

Invalid Date

Learning outcomes

By the end of this practical you should be able to:

  • Recognise from an outcome variable alone which member of the GLM family you need
  • Fit and interpret a binary logistic regression, and explain an odds ratio in plain English
  • Judge a classifier honestly, against a majority-class baseline rather than on accuracy alone
  • Fit a Poisson regression with an offset, and check it for overdispersion
  • Decide when a negative binomial or quasi-likelihood model is warranted
